Night Signal
============

A demo deck for presagent. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge, or
double-click serve.command (Mac) or serve.bat (Windows). Press ? for
the controls.

The drawing in assets/lens.svg and the clip in assets/beacon.mp4 were
made for this repository (MIT). The YouTube slide embeds Georges Méliès’
1902 film "A Trip to the Moon" (Le Voyage dans la Lune), which is in the
public domain. The embed uses the upload recorded by Wikidata Q244971
as video id GXwbM_vOvb0. The film is not bundled in this repo.
