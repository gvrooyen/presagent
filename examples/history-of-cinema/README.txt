A history of cinema
====================

A presagent demo. Open index.html in Chrome or Edge, or double-click
serve.command (Mac) or serve.bat (Windows). Press ? for the controls.

Three public-domain films are in assets/. Titles, years, source URLs,
licences, and trim ranges are in CREDITS.md. The Lumière and Méliès
excerpts are picture only. Steamboat Willie keeps its soundtrack.
